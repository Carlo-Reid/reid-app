# Notes for working on this app

## Carlo's standing rules (always follow)

- **Big when we can.** "We have lots of room and some users are elderly and do not see well, so big when we can is better" (Carlo, Nov 9). Use the room: big words, big buttons, one thing under the other instead of small boxes side by side. On Auto update the three steps of a paper (1 Who is it from? · 2 Order number (PO) · 3 Prices) are stacked full width in order. Each step has its own soft colour tone (1 blue, 2 teal, 3 sand: a strip down the left, the number square, a light wash) so they are easy to tell apart.

- **Save buttons are green whenever there's something to save.** Any button whose label starts with "Save" (Save, Save them all, Save the statement, Save note…) is a clear green (#1f9d4a, the same in every colour theme) while it can be pressed, and goes back to normal once it's pressed or greyed out. This is done app-wide by `greenSave()` in `index.html` (class `save-go`), so new Save buttons get it automatically. Keep new save buttons labelled "Save…" and don't override their colour. The older `greenSaveButtons()` "nothing changed" grey (`btn-clean`) must never win over `save-go`: a Save in a question box (askChoice, e.g. "Save it") has nothing typed around it and must still be green.

- **An update only shows after the app is refreshed, and the live app can lag.** Staff use the app at **https://reid-app.pages.dev** (Cloudflare Pages, built from `main`; GitHub Pages also builds but isn't what they open). Cloudflare marks a push "Deployed successfully" within seconds, but on Nov 9 the live address kept serving the older copy for about 13 minutes after several pushes close together. So: say "about 5 to 15 minutes", not "a minute or two"; tell Carlo to press the Refresh box at the top (it turns green: "There is a new update"; it loads a brand new copy with a `?v=` time stamp) or Ctrl+Shift+R; and put small changes into one push instead of several minutes apart. To check what is really live: in a browser on the site, `fetch('/?v='+Date.now(),{cache:'no-store'})` and look for a string from the change (the commit's Cloudflare check also lists that deployment's own address).

- **Tell Carlo as soon as what he asked for is done.** If something extra comes up (a failed sync, another bug), say his request is done first, then work on the extra and tell him about it. Never keep him waiting on extra work without saying so (Carlo, Nov 9).

- **The "nothing to do here" rule: do it on everything from now on, without being asked** (Carlo, Nov 9: "always do this even if I do not ask; if I do not want it I will ask you to remove it"). When someone clicks a section that has no purpose (a heading, a title bar, a line of plain words: anything that is not a button, box or link), the **happy Eco Man pops up in violet** and says "Nothing to do here. Keep scrolling down." If the same spot is hit **more than 10 times** it follows **the angry rule** (red angry Eco Man, red bubble). Code: `ECO_IDLE` (the list of such places: add every new heading/plain section to it), `ecoIdleRule`, `.eco-pop.eco-violet` in `index.html`. Eco Man's own bubbles with nothing to press use the angry rule from the first click (see Eco Man below). On Auto update it covers the dark blue paper headings, the yellow "What to do" strip, the step titles and the plain lines. **Every new screen or section I build gets this.**

- **The dance rule: also on everything from now on** (Carlo, Nov 9). When any do-nothing section is clicked **more than 20 times**, Eco Man dances in the middle of the screen for 10 seconds with the caption "I don't know what you're doing, so I am going to dance for you for 10 seconds and let the boss know." The boss is told: each dance is saved (who, where, when; `eco_dance_log`, table `eco_dances`, `sync/extra/238_eco_dances.sql`) and owners see the last week's dances in a violet "For the boss" box under the hello box on Auto update (`ecoBossNote`, `eco_dance_list`). So the full ladder for a do-nothing spot is: 1 to 10 clicks happy violet Eco Man (headings/plain sections) or confused then angry (his bubbles, hello box); more than 10 red and angry; more than 20 the dance, then the count starts again. Code: `ecoDance`, built into `ecoIdleRule` and `ecoAngryRule`.

- **The "silly" rule: also on everything from now on** (Carlo, Nov 9). In any section that has a box to type in, a click anywhere in that section but the box (or a button) makes the happy Eco Man pop up just above the box and say "Silly Carlo, you have to enter it in the box. 👇", with a green arrow pointing at the box and the box lit up in green for 5 seconds. More than 20 times = the dance. It is automatic for any `.eco-bubble`, `.sm-g3`, `.eco-key` or `.sm-unk-find` that holds a visible typing box (the bubble or small section the box sits in, not the whole step, so the other bubbles in that step keep their own rules) (the click handler near `ecoPop` in `index.html`; `.eco-arrow`, `.eco-here`): add new kinds of sections to that list. Drop-down lists do not count as a typing box.

- **Training (the 🎓 Training button in the top bar)** (Carlo, Nov 9). While it is on (it turns green, "Training on", and stays on after a refresh), the questions on a page come up **one at a time in a box in the middle of the screen**, with Back, Next question and Stop training, and a line that says "Document 2 of 5 · Question 3 of 7".
