## Carlo's standing rules (always follow)

- **Big when we can.** "We have lots of room and some users are elderly and do not see well, so big when we can is better" (Carlo, Nov 9). Use the room: big words, big buttons, one thing under the other instead of small boxes side by side. On Auto update the three steps of a paper (1 Who is it from? · 2 Order number (PO) · 3 Prices) are stacked full width in order. Each step has its own soft colour tone (1 blue, 2 teal, 3 sand: a strip down the left, the number square, a light wash) so they are easy to tell apart.

- **Save buttons are green whenever there's something to save.** Any button whose label starts with "Save" (Save, Save them all, Save the statement, Save note…) is a clear green (#1f9d4a, the same in every colour theme) while it can be pressed, and goes back to normal once it's pressed or greyed out. This is done app-wide by `greenSave()` in `index.html` (class `save-go`), so new Save buttons get it automatically. Keep new save buttons labelled "Save…" and don't override their colour. The older `greenSaveButtons()` "nothing changed" grey (`btn-clean`) must never win over `save-go`: a Save in a question box (askChoice, e.g. "Save it") has nothing typed around it and must still be green.

- **An update only shows after the app is refreshed, and the live app can lag.** Staff use the app at **https://reid-app.pages.dev** (Cloudflare Pages, built from `main`; GitHub Pages also builds but isn't what they open). Cloudflare marks a push "Deployed successfully" within seconds, but on Nov 9 the live address kept serving the older copy for about 13 minutes after several pushes close together. So: say "about 5 to 15 minutes", not "a minute or two"; tell Carlo to press the Refresh box at the top (it turns green: "There is a new update"; it loads a brand new copy with a `?v=` time stamp) or Ctrl+Shift+R; and put small changes into one push instead of several minutes apart. **To keep updates fast (Carlo, Nov 10): one push per request, with the notes (CLAUDE.md) in the same commit as the code; never a notes-only push right before or after, because every push makes Cloudflare build again and they wait in line. A push that only changes notes gets `[CF-Pages-Skip]` in its commit message so Cloudflare does not build it.** To check what is really live: in a browser on the site, `fetch('/?v='+Date.now(),{cache:'no-store'})` and look for a string from the change (the commit's Cloudflare check also lists that deployment's own address).

## Speed: why pages are slow, and what was done (Carlo, Nov 10: "the pages are taking too long to load or even open a new one")

- **The main cause is the size of the database computer, not the app's code.** The Supabase project runs on the smallest kind (about 1 GB of memory: `shared_buffers` 256 MB, `max_connections` 60) and the database is **3.5 GB** (1.2 GB of it scanned supplier pages in `supplier_doc_pages`, 0.8 GB `storage.objects`). The working data does not fit in memory, so many page queries take 2 to 4 seconds. Measured with `pg_stat_statements` (since Oct 17): `install_list` 2.5 s a call, `customer_find` 3.6 s, `sales_history_by` 3.2 s, `community_list` 2.3 s, `town_list` 2.0 s, `postal_zone_list` 1.9 s, `sales_list` 1.9 s, `pb_categories` 1.4 s, `customer_dupe_hint` 1.1 s, `bank_partner_match` 6.9 s. The sync's own jobs also hold it for a while each run: `_sync_after` 28 s, `_sinv_autolink` 77 s, `refresh_comm_sale_jobs` 5.5 s, `_staff_after_sync` 3.7 s. **A bigger database computer (Supabase compute add-on) is the one change that speeds up everything; it costs money each month, so it is Carlo's call and he does it in his Supabase account.** I offered it Nov 10; no answer yet.
- Done in code on Nov 10: the four map lists (`rpcCache`: postal zones, towns, communities, neighbourhoods) are fetched again in the background at most every 6 hours, not every 10 minutes; the update check asks only for the page's ETag (a HEAD request) every 15 seconds and reads the whole 3.8 MB page only when the tag changes; Auto update loads once, all at once.
- Next, if he wants more without paying: make the slow page queries cheaper one by one (start with `install_list`, `customer_find`, `pb_categories`, the sales lists), and move the scanned pages out of the database tables into file storage.
